whysho EQ1

This installer will set up the whysho EQ1 audio plug-in on your Mac.

The following components will be installed:

  • VST3 → /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
  • Audio Unit (AU) → /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components

After installation, restart your DAW (or rescan plug-ins) to use whysho EQ1.
